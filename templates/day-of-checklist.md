# Day-of Checklist

Print this, tape it to the kit box lid, and tick boxes as you go. It
covers the week before, the drive set-up, the fifty minutes, and
clean-up, so nothing lives only in your head. Chapter 1 gets the yes;
this page keeps it.

## The week before

- [ ] Confirmation email answered with date in bold, arrival time,
      and your needs (outlet, table count, chairs).
- [ ] The host's three questions answered and written into the visit
      file: projector input, microphone situation, who unlocks.
- [ ] Read-aloud passage printed at 14-point-plus and marked: slashes
      for breath, SLOW over names, STOP at the end.
- [ ] Handouts printed for large print, per
      [the accessibility rules](../docs/03-accessibility.md) -- 16 to
      18 point, high contrast, short lines.
- [ ] Demo rehearsed once on the actual laptop, once airplane-mode.
- [ ] Screenshots of every demo step saved to the USB stick too.
- [ ] Card reader tested at home with a 10-cent sale; cash box
      stocked to float levels (ones, fives, quarters).
- [ ] Book stock counted and written on the stock card; bookmarks
      and signup sheet in the box.
- [ ] Day-of route checked: parking, entrance, load-in door if any.

## The night before

- [ ] Laptop updated *and* sleep disabled (updates ambush projectors).
- [ ] Everything charged: laptop, phone, card reader.
- [ ] Box packed against the printable
      [repeatable kit inventory](../templates/repeatable-kit-inventory.md):
      adapters, extension cord, cloth, signage, tape, marker, mints.
- [ ] Water bottle filled; tomorrow treated like a flight day.

## Thirty minutes before guests

- [ ] Arrive early (30 minutes is the manual's rule; 45 for a first
      venue). Introduce yourself to the person who unlocked the door.
- [ ] Plug everything in once, walk to the back corner, and check
      readability from there.
- [ ] Raise the lights: faces and print visible, projector not the
      only light in the room.
- [ ] Lay the book table: cloth, face-out row, price card large,
      payment signs, free-bookmark sign, signup sheet on clipboard.
- [ ] Cable runs taped to an edge; nothing crosses a walking path.
- [ ] Water within reach at the speaking spot; phone silenced.
- [ ] Chair arc set or the five-minute invitation ready to invite
      people to shuffle closer.

## The session itself

- [ ] Start on time even if the room is half full -- latecomers join
      a working event instead of reshaping it.
- [ ] Welcome: what the hour looks like, invitation to move closer.
- [ ] Reading from the paper copy, one notch slower than natural.
- [ ] Five-minute story told between the reading and the demo.
- [ ] Demo at one tool, one task, narrated for eyes that cannot see
      the screen; airplane-mode fallback ready to go without drama.
- [ ] Every question repeated into the room before you answer it.
- [ ] Mic used if a mic exists.
- [ ] Punctual close at [END TIME]: thanks to guests, first-name
      thanks to staff, hand pointed to the table.
- [ ] Table open for another fifteen minutes while guests chat and
      the room resets.

## Clean-up and the drive home

- [ ] Room reset better than found: chairs back, cables coiled,
      trash walked out.
- [ ] Stock counted again; ledger updated the same night: date,
      venue, units, gross, fees.
- [ ] Box restocked checklist: what got used, what broke, what ran
      out (restock this week, not next event).
- [ ] Two or three guest impressions and the staff member's one
      answer written into the visit file while the room is still warm
      in memory.
- [ ] Thank-you note flagged in your reminders for tonight or
      tomorrow -- never next week.

## If something breaks

The manual line to remember when a thing fails: the projector dies,
hand out the prints; the Wi-Fi dies, run airplane-mode; the microphone
dies, face and repeat. Nobody remembers what broke at a good event.
